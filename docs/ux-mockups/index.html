MediChain — how the app will look

Mockups in the app's own dark-theme colours. "Before" shots are the live app today (fixture patient). Nothing is removed — every current page is reachable from one of the five tabs. Plan: docs/UX_SIMPLIFICATION_PLAN.md.

1 · Patient home and navigation

Bottom bar with five labelled tabs; Emergency card first; Messages badge counts unread items (the old bell dot only showed connection status).

2 · Every page still has a home

3 · Reading a record without the scrolling

Tap a record → the document opens directly (one sheet, never two). Findings first; the 9 empty fields become one line; Download stays pinned.

4 · Patient on a laptop

5 · Break-glass: one tap

Was: choose a reason, get refused, then type at least 10 characters. Now: tap a reason and the chart opens. Same 60-minute limit, same patient notice, same audit row.

6 · Shift handoff written by the shift

Patients you cared for are listed from your own records — Select all. Each SBAR is drafted from what you recorded, each line tagged with its source; you review and add to-dos.

7 · Forms: patient first, tap instead of type

Open the patient once; every task carries them. "Now" button for time, one-tap choices with nothing pre-selected, rarer fields folded away, score from the server with how much was measured.

8 · Patient first — the workspace

Choose the patient once; every form already knows them; "This visit" lists everything saved; Close patient checks drafts and offers the next patient.

9 · Ambulance to hospital — one button

The crew's taps build a time-stamped record; choosing the hospital sends the pre-alert; arrival is one button; the hospital sees it in ATMIST order and receives with one tap.

10 · Motion

Open motion-demo.html in a browser to try it live, including the reduced-motion version.
