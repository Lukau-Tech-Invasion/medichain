## Submission requirements and strategy
Internal preparation brief | Public sources checked 7 October 2026

The organiser LinkedIn listing announces entries closing Friday 23 October 2026 and the virtual hackathon running 23-25 October. The closing time and timezone, awards judging days and event date were not found. Submit before the final day and obtain the judging window separately. [S6]

### Three distinct programmes
The awards recognise an existing usable app; the hackathon has its own event conditions and themes; the Academy is the learning programme. Do not assume registration or eligibility in one grants entry to the others. [S1, S7, S16]

Enter Best Health Solution first. One category is free; an additional category costs R250 excluding VAT. Enterprise is a possible paid secondary entry only if the professional workflow is strongly qualified. Best Hackathon eligibility needs event-specific confirmation. [S4, S5]

Do not submit this access-incomplete draft as a finished entry. Resolve hosted access, current screenshots, factual identity and build proof. Keep all final materials consistent with the live demonstration.

## Requirements ledger
Required fields, organiser guidance and our recommendations have different status.

### Form fields carrying required markers
Name; company; email; phone; category; app name; publication location; African development location/country; invoice name/address/billing email; terms/privacy acceptance. Billing fields appear even for a free first category. Verify the final live form for conditional changes. [S3]

### Other displayed fields
Telephone; app description; icon; APK/app links; price; supporting files; demo username/password/login process; VAT number; marketing consent. Absence of a visible required marker does not remove the practical need for a useful description and judge access. [S3]

### Official functional requirements
Judges must be able to use a complete solution. Store publication is not essential when remote access and login details are supplied. The FAQ permits screenshot/image upload. [S1]

### Upload limits remain unknown
The supporting-file input allows multiple selection, with no exposed accept filter or required attribute. Maximum size, number, server validation and preferred file formats were not displayed. Do not test by submitting or uploading private documents. [S3]

### PWA publication route
The publication field provides Other. Use the verified hosted browser URL and describe the patient/staff web experience accurately. Obtain explicit organiser confirmation of PWA/device expectations before relying on that route. [S3]

### Eligibility and conditional documents
Review promoter/sponsor affiliation exclusions and accurate-entry obligations. Travel documents concern a travel prize; they are not a universal entry upload. The promoter may request further information. Do not invent documents or entitlement to enter. [S5]

## How judging should shape the pack
Published domains; no public numeric weighting located.

### Published process
The FAQ describes two rounds, hands-on app exploration in round one, and three finalists per category. It lists product, creativity, usability, sustainability, impact, presentation and intelligence. [S1]

### Our evidence response
Working role transitions answer product and usability. Patient-visible access and care continuity support creativity. A buyer proposition and operating model address sustainability. Impact requires measured results when available. A concise connected journey supports presentation. Explainable rules address current intelligence honestly.

### Intelligence needs discipline
The FAQ includes forecasting/pattern-based behaviour, but the current pack does not pretend rule-based scoring is trained predictive AI. Explain current capability and validation; show a future predictive feature only as a future proposal.

### Judge insight, used carefully
The organiser-attributed 2025 release highlights real-world constraints, offline support and accessibility in judge commentary. Treat these as historical qualitative guidance, not 2026 weights. Demonstrate accessible text size now; measure offline/low-data before making claims. [S12]

### Competitive context
Vula reports 2025 overall and health wins; health is a serious competitive category. Our response is a clear patient-to-care-team continuity proposition and proof, not unsupported market uniqueness. [S11]

### Roster and influence
The official roster lists judges but does not label its year. Do not imply personal endorsement or design an entry around guessed individual preferences. No judge was contacted. [S9]

## A persuasive submission bundle
Recommended packaging, subject to the organiser upload rules.

### 01 | Product submission
Seven pages selling the application through patient agency, care-team continuity, trust and a focused adoption hypothesis. Lead upload once factual identity and current proof are confirmed.

### 02 | Capability evidence
Nine pages explaining every capability family, its benefit, source presence, demonstration and current qualification boundary. Use as supporting evidence rather than overwhelming the main story.

### 03 | Judge guide
Three pages with a suggested ten-minute route. Replace access-status wording with verified URL/account/support instructions before uploading. Put passwords in private form fields, not public marketing PDFs.

### 04 | Pitch and video script
Six concise pages for a recorded or live pitch. A three-minute video is recommended, not required by a published rule. Record real current app screens and label synthetic data.

### 05 | Research and requirements
This internal brainstorming brief stays with the team. Its editable inventory, claims ledger and release checklist support later revisions. None of this pack is the final submission.

### Media and evidence assets
Capture six current synthetic screenshots and a current screen recording. Use a recognisable icon and consistent name. Keep this workshop edition focused on product structure; external user and pilot material is deferred. Do not attach identity/legal documents merely because the upload is vague.

## Work backwards from 23 October
Internal targets; organiser closing time still needs confirmation.

### 7-10 October | Facts and scope
Confirm submitter/company, publication status, category and rights to the app. Ask the organiser about PWA eligibility, mandatory files/limits and judging dates. Select the core demonstration and record known limitations.

### 10-14 October | Hosting and durability
Create the new AWS account, confirm Free Plan service access, provision the authorised architecture separately, verify HTTPS and backups. Qualify separate-role sessions and all five headline journeys on the actual hosted build.

### 14-18 October | Product proof
Capture clean synthetic screenshots and a full successful walkthrough. Record timings only if measured. Resolve or narrow failed paths and make the claims ledger match the build.

### 18-21 October | Submission finish
Confirm final factual sign-off, add private login details, compress files within confirmed limits, review the mobile experience, test every link from a separate network and submit early. Save entry ID, uploaded versions and any fee receipt.

### 22-23 October | Contingency
Keep time for portal failures or organiser clarification. Do not assume midnight or use the hackathon end date as the awards entry deadline.

### During judging
Keep URLs, credentials, fixture data and funding stable through the organiser-confirmed window. Preserve support availability, backups and the exact submitted release.

## Research record and source hierarchy
Public material reviewed; relevant guidance retained and historical rules separated.

### Authoritative first
Entry page and linked form, FAQ, categories, 2026 terms, contact page, current hackathon themes, organiser LinkedIn and sponsor FNB article. The form was inspected read-only with no personal entry or submission.

### Historical and comparative
Official winner archive; Vula primary winner story and public LinkedIn announcement; organiser-attributed 2025 judge commentary; 2025 programme; a public previous entrant pack; Academy terms. Historical examples inform packaging only.

### Conflicts surfaced
The FAQ still contains 2025 Academy copy while the form says 2026. The category page and form can differ. LinkedIn gives current October dates while the main terms do not specify a deadline. Recheck final form and organiser messages before submission.

### Excluded material
Older MTN-era rules, unrelated banking complaints, unrelated app awards and generic advice are not treated as 2026 rules. An entrant pack does not establish acceptance or judge preference. Academy completion is not assumed from repository history.

### Coverage boundary
The search covered organiser, sponsor, winner, judge commentary and public social/entrant material across targeted rounds. It cannot establish that every internet page or future update has been found. The practical remaining information is recorded as organiser questions.

### Evidence distinction
External sources establish competition facts. Repository files establish implementation presence. Dated local runs establish selected observed behaviour. Clinical or commercial impact needs separate real-world evidence.
