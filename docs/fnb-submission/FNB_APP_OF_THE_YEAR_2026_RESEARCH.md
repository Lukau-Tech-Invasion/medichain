## 1. Bottom line

1. **The deadline is probably Friday 23 October 2026, four days from now.** That date comes from one source only: the organiser's LinkedIn page, as found in the 7 October research. I could not re-open that page on 19 October (LinkedIn blocks automated reads), and the official website publishes no closing date. Treat 23 October as real until the organisers say otherwise. Phone 011 467 3341 on Monday 19 October to confirm the date and the closing time.
2. **There is no official document checklist.** The entry is one online form (Cognito). Its "Supporting Documents" field accepts several files of any type and has no stated list, size or format. Every past entrant has had to decide for themselves what to upload.
3. **Judges use the app itself first.** In Round 1 they open each app on their own devices and score it. Finalists then do technical judging and a live pitch. A demo judges can reach and use counts for more than any PDF.
4. **Health apps win here.** Vula Medical won both Best Health and the overall title in 2025, and Kena Health did the same in 2023. Every Best Health winner since 2020 was a live product with real users.
5. **MediChain's two biggest gaps:**
   - **No traction.** Vula showed 2.5 million referrals and Kena 300,000+ downloads; MediChain has no users or pilot yet.
   - **No hosted demo yet.** It is planned on AWS, after the current testing.
6. **The form's "Where is your App published" question offers only app stores.** As of 19 October it lists only Google Play, the Apple App Store and Huawei AppGallery, and the answer is required. MediChain is a web app (PWA). The 7 October research saw an "Other" option, but it was not there on 19 October. Ask the organisers how to answer for a web app.

### 4.4 The closest peer: iER
- **What it is:** a free South African emergency app by Affinity App / Affinity Life.
- **Features:** a panic button and silent alerts, sharing location, photos and voice notes with a 24/7 contact centre, infrastructure reporting, sign-in by phone number and one-time PIN, and **storing the user's personal and medical information for emergencies**.
- **Why it matters:** judges have already rewarded "emergency information for responders". MediChain must show what it adds:
  - clinician-verified data rather than self-reported;
  - an emergency read that works offline, from an NFC card or QR code;
  - a record of who opened the data, when and why, visible to the patient;
  - a full care record behind the emergency summary.

### 6.2 Product (from README.md and CLAUDE.md)
- **Pitch line:** "A paramedic taps an NFC card and sees blood type, allergies and DNR status in under three seconds — with no internet connection."
- **Patients see access:** every read is logged with who, why, when and which fields were shown, and the patient can see it.
- **Who can open a record:** care relationships and break-glass emergency access decide it. Break-glass needs a live work context, an approved device and a grant issued by the server. NFC tokens are short-lived and signed.
- **Consent model:** the POPIA legal-basis model covers §11 grounds, §32 health authorisation, §34–35 children's information and Children's Act §129 mature minors.
- **Audit stream:** sealed (Merkle-batched, hash-chained, signed with Ed25519, exported as checkpoints). The blockchain was removed by ADR-0010 on 29 September 2026.
- **Encryption:** ChaCha20-Poly1305 for documents and Argon2id for credentials.
- **Seven roles:** patient, doctor, nurse, lab technician, pharmacist, paramedic and admin.
- **Two web apps:** a doctor portal (151 pages) and a patient app (53 pages, with a six-language switcher; additional languages are marked "coming soon").
- **Expo mobile patient app core** in `mobile-examples/expo-starter`: emergency card, records, family, biometric unlock. Typechecks, but has not been built into an APK.
- **Clinical decision support is rule-based:** qSOFA, drug-interaction checks and lab-trend detection. **There is no predictive model.** `lab_trends.rs` returns `prediction: None`.

### 6.3 Evidence of quality (counts need re-measuring on the AWS build; see C8)

## 9. Blockers before submitting

1. **Confirm the deadline** (section 1 and C1).
2. **Answer the app-store question** (C2). If the organisers won't accept a PWA, the fallbacks are:
   - build the Expo patient app into an APK for the APK field;
   - wrap the PWA as a Play Store app (Play review takes days, which the deadline may not allow).
3. **A hosted demo judges can reach:** a public HTTPS URL on AWS, kept up through judging.
4. **Survives a restart:** after a reboot the API can fall back to an empty in-memory store while `/health` still reports healthy (DEMO_READINESS_REPORT §2.0a). Make `/health` fail in that state, or monitor a real login.
5. **Judge accounts:** dedicated synthetic accounts; never the `pitch.*` or super-admin credentials. Several accounts in one browser are not supported (one origin per account), so tell judges to use a private window per role, or provide separate links.
6. **Synthetic data only**, labelled "Fictional data" on screen; rate limiting stays on.

### 9.1 AWS notes (summary of the 7 October hosting research)
- **Recommended for South African hosting:** EC2 `t3.small` (2 GB) in **Cape Town (`af-south-1`)**, which is Free Plan eligible. **Lightsail has no Cape Town region.** The cheapest Lightsail option elsewhere is 2 GB at $12/month.
- **Free Plan:** $100 credit (up to $200), ending after 6 months or when credits run out. It is not a permanent free host.
- **One host** running Nginx, the React apps, the Rust API and PostgreSQL. S3 holds backups and audit-checkpoint copies. Only ports 443 (and 80 for redirects) are public. Encrypted EBS; least-privilege role; IMDSv2.
- **Build images before copying them to the server;** a 2 GB host will struggle compiling Rust.
- **Config traps:**
  - the awards override binds Nginx to loopback;
  - the production override does not publish port 80;
  - the off-site backup override points to Azure Blob;
  - S3 is not yet a native checkpoint sink;
  - `GRAFANA_ADMIN_PASSWORD` is needed if the monitoring profile is on.
- **Turn off dev and bootstrap conveniences** on any public host.
- **Avoid Spot instances for the judging server,** because an interruption ends access.
