# 04 MediChain Pitch and Video Script

Prepared 7 October 2026. Research and positioning workshop. Not a final submission.

## Care that carries forward.
MediChain | Best Health Solution | Proposed three-minute pitch

### Opening | 0:00-0:25
A patient arrives with a medical story. Every new clinician, shift or emergency responder needs the right part of that story. MediChain gives the patient and the care team connected ways to carry it forward.

### Show the application
Open the patient record, then the staff chart for the same synthetic patient. Show the actual interface immediately. Avoid a long problem-only introduction.

### The memorable promise
One patient context. Role-specific work. Patient-visible accountability.

## The patient has a place in the journey
Scene 2 | 0:25-0:55

### Narration
The patient can inspect records and observations, organise appointments and medicines, and communicate with the care team. Consent controls and recorded access history make information use visible.

### Show
A stored record, one recorded access reason and the consent interface. Keep synthetic-data identification visible.

### Sell the value
This is agency and continuity a person can interact with. Do not claim measured engagement or adherence improvements without data.

## The care team continues the same story
Scene 3 | 0:55-1:35

### Narration
The clinician sees the chart and reviewed diagnostic evidence. Pharmacy has prescription processing and history. Nursing care and SBAR handover keep the next shift informed. Each role has a task-focused view of the same patient context.

### Show
Reviewed lab result in the chart; prescription history; incoming nurse handover. Use currently passing journeys and refresh one view to demonstrate persistence.

### Sell the value
MediChain connects the work around the encounter. The broader application also includes specialist, procedure, imaging, preventive and administrative capabilities; the evidence appendix explains their readiness.

## Urgent context, accountable access
Scene 4 | 1:35-2:05

### Narration
In the responder experience, medical identity and authorised emergency access can surface essential context and carry the patient into ambulance handover. Afterwards, the patient can inspect the recorded disclosure.

### Show
Synthetic browser tag scan, scoped demo responder device, patient association and disclosure history. Label the scan as simulated; physical NFC is a separate validation task.

### Sell the value
The product pairs useful information with a visible record of its use. Emergency convenience and patient trust belong in the same story.

## A product with a focused next step
Scene 5 | 2:05-2:35

### Narration
The commercial proposition is a facility offering that connects records, diagnostics-to-treatment and handover. The next positioning decision is how to package these capabilities with transparent onboarding, support and operating cost.

### Show
Three connected product tasks: retrieve the record; follow diagnostic evidence into treatment; continue the incoming shift. Use actual app views once hosting is ready.

### Sell the value
A coherent facility offering and an operating-cost plan make the application discussable as a sustainable product.

## Judge the journey. Inspect the evidence.
Closing | 2:35-3:00

### Narration
MediChain brings patient agency, care-team work and emergency continuity into connected experiences. Explore the patient journey and the transitions between roles. Then inspect the evidence, current limits and the next validation milestone.

### Final screen
App name, Best Health Solution category, verified hosted URL once issued, and the three words: Continuity. Agency. Accountability.

### Recording checklist
Use the exact submitted release and synthetic fixtures. Add captions; use readable zoom; keep passwords off-screen; ensure audio is clear. Export a small accessible video and test the viewing link privately before submitting.

### Pitch status
This is a proposed script and presentation aid. It does not assert that a video or a three-minute pitch is an official submission requirement.
